Healthcare organizations are under pressure to deliver high-quality patient care while managing economic uncertainty, changing regulations, rising costs, and growing operational demands. At the same time, clinicians and leaders must make decisions using data that is often spread across electronic health records, core business systems, claims platforms, and outside data sources.
