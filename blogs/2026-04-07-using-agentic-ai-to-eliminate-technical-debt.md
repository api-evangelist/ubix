The press has been buzzing on the dramatic impact AI is having (and will have) on the nature of enterprise work. From predictions that “ all white collar work will be automated by AI in 18 months ” to the asymmetric impact of generative AI has on job post availability, media is pushing boards and executive teams to reprioritize IT spend towards AI transformation projects.
