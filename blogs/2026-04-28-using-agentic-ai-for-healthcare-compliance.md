Every industry has been impacted by AI transformation projects and for healthcare, the AI transformative possibilities range from agentic diagnostics and treatment to greater operational efficiencies and regulatory compliance support. With current research projecting that “artificial intelligence in healthcare market size was estimated at USD 36.67 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach USD 505.59 billion by 2033, growing at a CAGR of 38.90% from 2026 to 2033”, it should come at no surprise that a significant portion of this spend is focused on dealing with the ongoing challenge of keeping up with regulations and compliance requirements.
