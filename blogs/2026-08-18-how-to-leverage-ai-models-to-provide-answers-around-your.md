AI is becoming ubiquitous but completely dependent upon data for accuracy instead of hallucination . For businesses to successfully mobilize AI transformation, this becomes the paramount challenge.Instead of relying on weak models built exclusively from publicly available data, the question becomes how to ensure that AI models provide the answers you are looking for by leveraging and contextualizing your data while at the same time protecting it from being exploited by the AI models outside of your organization.
