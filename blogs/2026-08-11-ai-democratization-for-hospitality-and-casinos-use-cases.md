For hospitality and casino executives, the value of AI is shifting from experimentation to measurable business impact. Many organizations still judge digital performance by traffic, clicks, and impressions, but those metrics do not necessarily translate into profitable guests, direct bookings, or higher lifetime value. AI helps leaders move beyond activity-based reporting by identifying which channels, audiences, and experiences actually drive revenue and margin.
