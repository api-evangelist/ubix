The rapid advancement of GenAI and synthetic media technologies has made deepfakes increasingly realistic, scalable, and difficult to detect using traditional fraud, security, and content verification tools. Organizations face growing exposure to digital identity fraud, executive impersonation, synthetic claims, payment fraud, reputational damage, regulatory scrutiny, and financial losses.
