AI is rapidly becoming a strategic priority for retailers and Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG) companies, but value does not come from experimentation alone. The biggest risks for AI transformation are not the algorithms themselves; they are fragmented strategy, poor data quality, legacy technology constraints, cost uncertainty, talent gaps, ethical exposure, and weak change management.
