Financial Planning & Analysis ( FP&A ) is required by corporations today to move beyond just survival mode to sustainable growth mode and competitive dominance. Acting as the bridge between financial data and strategic decision-making and providing forward-looking insights, forecasting, budgeting, and performance analysis, FP&A enables companies to navigate economic changes, optimize resources, and drive profitability, distinguishing itself from retroactive accounting. Unlike accounting, which records historical data, FP&A is forward-looking, utilizing data to guide company strategy, optimize resource allocation, and project future performance
