City planners looking to modernization operations with AI have mostly efforts failed because available data remains fragmented, reporting remains manual, and analytics remain inaccessible to the average, nontechnical user. This is primarily due to siloed data and inflexible sharing across departments which leads to manual, slow, and costly compliance and reporting processes.
